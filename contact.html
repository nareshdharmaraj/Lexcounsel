Client FAQ.

What is your typical response time?

We aim to acknowledge all new inquiries within 4 business hours. Complex case reviews typically receive a preliminary strategy within 24-48 hours.

Do you offer fixed-fee arrangements?

Yes, for specific transactional matters or initial discovery phases, we provide transparent, flat-fee structures to ensure budget predictability.

How do you secure client data?

We utilize military-grade end-to-end encryption on our proprietary client portal, compliant with SOC2 and GDPR standards.

Can you handle cross-border disputes?

Absolutely. Our multi-jurisdictional task forces are equipped to manage litigation and arbitration across over 35 jurisdictions globally.
