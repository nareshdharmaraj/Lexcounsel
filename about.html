Legacy.

Founded in 2001, LexCounsel was built on the principle that elite legal advocacy should be as dynamic as the markets it serves. Over two decades, we have evolved from a boutique litigation house into a global powerhouse, representing over 65% of the Fortune 500. Our legacy is defined by a relentless commitment to excellence and a deep understanding of the law's role in shaping the corporate and private landscapes of tomorrow.
